# Youtube-Bypass

Watch YouTube with your ad blocker enabled, without getting stuck on the "Ad blockers are not allowed" screen.

## What it does

YouTube Bypass watches for YouTube's ad blocker warning while you're watching a video. When the warning appears, the extension automatically refreshes the page and puts you back at the same timestamp you were at, so you pick up right where you left off instead of starting the video over.

- Detects YouTube's ad blocker warning automatically
- Refreshes the page for you, with no clicking required
- Restores your playback position after the refresh
- Works alongside your existing ad blocker

## Installation

YouTube Bypass isn't on the Chrome Web Store, so you'll load it manually. It works in Google Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, Opera, etc.).

1. **Download the extension.** Click the green **Code** button on this page and choose **Download ZIP**, or fork/clone the repository.
2. **Extract it** (if you downloaded the ZIP) and save the folder somewhere permanent on your computer. Chrome loads the extension from this folder, so don't delete or move it afterward.
3. **Open the extensions page.** Type `chrome://extensions` into your address bar and press Enter.
4. **Turn on Developer mode** using the toggle in the top-right corner.
5. **Click "Load unpacked,"** then browse to the extension folder, open it, and click **Select Folder**.

The extension loads immediately and starts working the next time you open a YouTube video.

## Updating

Download the newest version, replace the old folder's contents with it, then click the **reload** icon on the extension's card in `chrome://extensions`.
